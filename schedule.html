Ethical Vibe Coding — course at a glance

“Ethical vibe coding” is almost an oxymoron — vibe coding is brain-off, let-the-AI-drive; ethics is brain-on, slow and deliberate. This course is the experiment in marrying them: build fast with AI while staying awake to the harms, the values, and the people on the other side. Every day pairs a short lecture with a hands-on build; each week ends in a group project; the final ships something that benefits a real person outside the class.
