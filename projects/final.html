Each day is graded on Canvas as the team progresses. The summary of the four checkpoints is your final-project grade (25%), plus a separate Final Presentation grade (15%). An anonymous peer-check adjusts for unfair contribution.

Decide which reading of your value you designed for (privacy as control vs. security vs. contextual integrity — the reading decides the method), research what's already been tried, run the check, and report what works and what doesn't across the three lenses. Full Day-1 brief →
