# Team Peer Check (anonymous)

A short, **anonymous** peer assessment run **after each group project** (Project 2 and
the Final; a light version can also close out Project 1).

**Grades stay at the team level.** We grade the *composition* of the work — knowledge,
skill, creativity, and how you distributed and orchestrated it across people and AI — so
this check does **not** produce individual scores. It's an early-warning signal: it helps
us spot an unfair split so we can help teams rebalance, and it informs the **rare,
exceptional case** (a member who was essentially absent) where an individual grade is
justified. Most teams will never see it affect a grade.

**Recommended delivery:** an anonymous Google Form or Canvas quiz, one submission per
student, listing their own teammates. Keep it to ~3 minutes. Below are the questions to
paste in.

---

## Questions

**1. Your team** — [dropdown of team names]

**2. Rate each teammate's overall contribution** (leave yourself out).
For each teammate, pick one:
- **Carried more than their share** — did notably more than an equal part
- **Fair share** — pulled their weight
- **Below their share** — contributed noticeably less
- **Little to none** — largely absent or non-contributing

**3. Was the workload split fair overall?**
- Yes, fair
- Mostly fair, minor imbalance
- No, significantly unfair

**4. Did everyone get a real chance to drive the AI / take the keyboard?**
- Yes · Somewhat · No

**5. Anything we should know?** (optional, free text — conflicts, someone who
went above and beyond, access problems, a teammate who was excused/absent, etc.)

**6. Confirm** — "My answers are honest and my own." ☐

---

## How we use it

- **First use is to help, not to grade** — a flagged imbalance is a prompt for us to
  check in and help the team rebalance while the project is still live.
- The team grade is the default for everyone. We split out an individual grade only in an
  **exceptional case** — a member essentially absent — and only when the peer signal is
  **consistent** (multiple teammates independently) *and* matches git history and the AI
  log. A single outlier response never changes a grade.
- Excused/known absences (see the attendance policy) are accounted for — an excused
  teammate is not penalized for time they were away, and teammates are not penalized for
  covering that gap.
